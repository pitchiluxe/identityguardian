# Interface design

Proposed enterprise analyst workspace: navy surfaces, restrained cyan actions/focus, amber warnings, red critical findings with text/icons. System sans-serif; monospace for IDs. Spacing 4/8/12/16/24/32 px, 8 px card corners, visible borders. Target WCAG 2.2 AA with tested contrast, keyboard navigation, reduced motion and non-color severity.

Persistent header shows organization, LAB/SANDBOX/PRODUCTION, freshness, search and user. Navigation:

- Overview: Dashboard, Identities, Applications, Access, Identity Graph.
- Intelligence: Privilege Radar, Attack Paths, Investigations, Time Machine.
- Governance: Access Reviews, JIT Access, Lifecycle, What-If Simulator, Policies.
- Non-human: Machine Identities, AI Agents.
- Learning: Labs.
- Operations: Reports, Audit Logs, Integrations, Administration, AI Assistant.

Unimplemented pages are omitted or clearly PLANNED. Every control works or is explicitly DEMO/PLANNED. Dashboard numbers derive from one scoped snapshot; no random production statistics. Empty, loading, stale, partial, denied, unavailable and failed states are distinct.

Desktop layout: summaries, central table/graph/workflow, expandable evidence panel. Identity profile shows accounts, access, lineage, risk story, timeline and ownership. Graph is selectable and filterable with depth limits; keyboard-accessible relationship table provides equivalent data.

Review panel shows role/department/manager, entitlement source, usage coverage/frequency, peers/sample size, history, ticket, justification and uncertainty. REMOVE creates a proposal. Wizard separates simulation, review, independent approval, execution and recovery; shows exact targets/environment and unknown dependencies.

Time Machine distinguishes effective and knowledge times with timezone/coverage. AI claims link evidence. Lab separates tasks, configuration, validation, hints and report. Exports disclose scope and redaction. Visual layout is a specification, not a fabricated product screenshot.
